Privacy Policy

The Canadian Gem Trade Guild may collect information submitted through membership applications, contact forms, newsletter registrations and direct communications.

Information we may collect

This may include names, business details, contact information, membership records, sourcing preferences and documents voluntarily submitted for review.

How information is used

Information may be used to review applications, administer memberships, communicate with members, improve Guild services and provide relevant trade updates.

Sharing and protection

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the website, process payments or deliver communications, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to legal and operational requirements. Newsletter subscribers may unsubscribe at any time.

Terms of Membership

Membership is subject to application review, payment of applicable fees and continued compliance with Guild standards.

Eligibility and review

The Guild may request information reasonably required to assess an applicant. Submission of an application does not guarantee approval.

Fees and renewals

Membership fees, renewal periods and included benefits will be stated at purchase. Unless otherwise stated, fees are non-refundable after membership activation.

Member responsibilities

Members must provide accurate information, communicate professionally and avoid misleading statements about gemstones, treatments, origins, pricing or business capabilities.

No transaction guarantee

Membership, verification and introductions do not guarantee sales, purchases, valuation, authenticity or commercial results. Members remain responsible for independent due diligence and agreements.

Suspension or termination

The Guild may suspend or end membership for non-payment, inaccurate information, misconduct, misuse of Guild branding or conduct that may harm other members or the organization.
