About the Guild

A stronger, more trustworthy gem trade ecosystem.

The Canadian Gem Trade Guild is designed to create a trusted bridge between gemstone suppliers and buyers. Membership helps establish credibility, improve communication and provide a more organized route into the Canadian market.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide through membership applications, contact forms, newsletter signups or direct communications, including your name, business name, email address, phone number and information about your gemstone or jewellery business.

How we use information

We use this information to review membership applications, communicate with members and applicants, provide Guild services, improve our website, maintain records and send updates that you have requested.

Payments and third-party services

Payments and certain forms may be processed by trusted third-party providers. Those providers handle information under their own privacy policies. We do not intend to store full payment-card details on this website.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers when needed to operate the Guild, process applications or comply with legal obligations.

Data retention and protection

We retain information only for as long as reasonably required for membership administration, business records and legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements. You may also unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at info@canadiangemtradeguild.ca or through WhatsApp at +1 343 297 7219.

Terms of Membership

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Membership eligibility

Membership is available to applicants who meet the eligibility requirements for the selected membership category and provide complete, accurate and current information. Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance.

Application and review

The Canadian Gem Trade Guild may review business information, references, documents and other materials reasonably required to assess an application. We may request additional information, approve an application, decline it or place it on hold at our discretion.

Membership fees

Membership fees, billing periods and included benefits will be shown before payment. Unless otherwise stated in writing, fees are non-transferable and are not refundable once an application review or membership period has begun.

Member responsibilities

Members must act honestly and professionally, follow applicable laws, accurately describe gemstones and jewellery, disclose known treatments or material facts, respect confidential information and avoid misleading, fraudulent or abusive conduct.

Verification and Guild status

Guild membership or verification reflects the information reviewed at a particular time. It is not a guarantee of any member, gemstone, transaction, product quality, legal compliance, financial performance or future conduct.

Use of Guild name and materials

Members may use approved membership badges, directory listings or Guild materials only in the manner authorized by the Guild. Membership does not transfer ownership of the Guild name, logo, website content or other intellectual property.

Buyer connections and transactions

The Guild may facilitate introductions, visibility or access to trade opportunities, but it does not guarantee buyer interest, sales, pricing, payment or transaction completion. Members remain responsible for their own due diligence, agreements, taxes, shipping, insurance and legal obligations.

Suspension or termination

Membership may be suspended, restricted or terminated for non-payment, false information, misuse of Guild materials, serious complaints, unlawful conduct, conduct that may harm the Guild or its members, or breach of these terms. Where appropriate, the member may be given an opportunity to respond.

Changes to membership

Membership benefits, fees, standards and these terms may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the website, email or another reasonable method. Continued membership after an update takes effect constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

Limitation of responsibility

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Guild is not responsible for indirect losses, lost profits, failed transactions or disputes between members, buyers, suppliers or third parties arising from membership, introductions or use of Guild services.
